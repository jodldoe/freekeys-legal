# FreeKey Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to **freekeys@agencyagency.at** and you will get a reply as soon as possible.

## Common questions

**The app does not hear my piano.**
Open Settings in the app (gear icon) and set the microphone sensitivity to High. Put the phone close to the piano, and check that FreeKey is allowed to use the microphone in the iOS Settings app under FreeKey.

**How do I connect a MIDI keyboard?**
Open a lesson, tap the icon at the top right, choose "MIDI keyboard", then "Connect Bluetooth MIDI…". USB keyboards connect automatically.

**Improvise does nothing when I play.**
Improvise only works with a MIDI keyboard. It does not listen through the microphone.

**I want to change my ad privacy choice.**
Open Settings in the app and tap "Ad privacy choices…". This entry is shown where the law requires a choice, for example in the EU.

**I left a tip by mistake.**
Tips are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
