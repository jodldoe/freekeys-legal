# FreeKey Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

FreeKey is published by **Agency Agency** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the FreeKey app for iOS uses and what happens to it.

Contact: **freekeys@agencyagency.at**

## Summary

- FreeKey has no accounts and we do not collect, store or receive any personal data from the app.
- Sound from your microphone is analysed on your device and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
- The app shows advertising from Google AdMob. Google processes some data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first.

## Data that stays on your device

**Microphone.** If you choose microphone input, FreeKey listens to your piano to check which notes you play. The audio is analysed in memory on your device as you play. It is not recorded, saved or transmitted, and it is not shared with the advertising service. You can use the app without the microphone by choosing a MIDI keyboard or the on-screen keys.

**Bluetooth and MIDI.** If you connect a MIDI keyboard, FreeKey reads the notes you play from it. This data is used only on your device.

**Progress and settings.** Which lessons you have completed and your settings (input method, metronome, sensitivity) are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

FreeKey displays banner ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited (for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) or Google LLC (elsewhere).

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process:

- your IP address, and the approximate location derived from it
- device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
- advertising-related identifiers and similar identifiers stored on your device
- how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information from the ad component

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. We see only aggregated statistics, such as the number of ads shown.

FreeKey does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

**Your choice.** In regions where consent is required, including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, FreeKey shows a consent form from Google when you first open the app. Personalised advertising is used only if you consent. If you decline, you may see non-personalised ads or no ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app under Settings, "Ad privacy choices…".

The legal basis for this processing is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). More information: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Tips (in-app purchases)

Tips are optional purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple provides us only with aggregated sales reports. Apple's handling of your data is described in the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

FreeKey is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google using the links above; you can also write to us at the contact address and we will help where we can. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
